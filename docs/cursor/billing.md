# Billing

How to access billing, change plans, manage seats, and handle invoices. For **plans and usage** (Pro, Teams, limits, on-demand), see [Pricing](./pricing.md).

**Use this doc when:** You need to open billing, switch monthly/annual, manage Teams seats, get invoices, update payment info, or cancel.

**For portal and current options:** [Cursor Dashboard](https://cursor.com/dashboard) → Billing, and [Cursor docs – Billing](https://docs.cursor.com/account/billing) if available.

---

## Access billing

**[Dashboard](https://cursor.com/dashboard)** → **Billing** in the sidebar. This opens the billing portal for subscriptions, payment, and invoices.

---

## Billing cycles

Cycles are **monthly** or **annual**, starting on your subscription date. **Teams:** charged per seat; new members are **pro-rated** for the rest of the cycle.

---

## Teams: seats

- One **seat per member**. Adding a member mid-cycle = charge for the remaining period only.
- If a member has used credits and is **removed**, their seat stays **occupied until the end of the billing cycle** (no pro-rated refund).
- Admins manage seats in the **Dashboard**.

---

## Switch monthly ↔ annual

**Pro**

1. [Dashboard](https://cursor.com/dashboard) → **Billing and Invoices** (left sidebar).
2. **Manage subscription** → **Update subscription**.
3. Choose **Yearly** or **Monthly** → **Continue**.

**Teams**

1. Dashboard → **Billing and Invoices**.
2. **Upgrade Now** to switch to yearly billing.

**Monthly → yearly** can be done self-serve. **Yearly → monthly** requires contacting [hi@cursor.com](mailto:hi@cursor.com).

---

## Invoices

- **View and download:** Billing portal → billing history (current and past invoices).
- **Auto-email:** Not yet available; invoices are downloaded manually. Opt-in for email invoices will be added later.

---

## Update billing information

In the billing portal you can update: **payment method**, **company name**, **address**, **tax information**. Payments are processed via **Stripe**. Changes apply only to **future** invoices; past invoices cannot be changed.

---

## Cancel subscription

**Billing and Invoices** → **Manage Subscription** → **Cancel subscription**. Access continues until the **end of the current billing period**.

---

## Other billing help

For issues not covered here: email [hi@cursor.com](mailto:hi@cursor.com) from the **email on your account** and include account details and the problem.
