The Problem

Emerging artists struggle to earn meaningful income on traditional streaming platforms.

Revenue depends on scale rather than direct fan support.

ARIA was designed to enable direct monetization from core fans.

The Solution

Built a streaming platform with:

Music Playback

Seamless streaming experience across all devices.

Library Management

Organize and manage your music collection.

Artist Subscriptions

Monthly subscriptions to support favorite artists.

Direct Tipping

Send tips directly to artists you love.

Goal-Based Funding

Support artists in achieving specific creative goals.

Secure Authentication

Protected accounts with role-based access.

Artist and Listener Roles

Distinct experiences tailored for creators and fans.
